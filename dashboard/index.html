How to read the feed

Implied value = TVL × 0.30 × velocity × overdue × breadth. Velocity is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50…+150%. Overdue grows with the days a protocol has run with no token (up to 900 days). Breadth adds 4% per chain, up to 5 chains. Signal age is days from first listing to the snapshot date. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued — it prices what the launch would be worth if the record stayed as it is.

HOT — daily velocity ≥ 1.5%: act within hoursWARM — 7-day change above zero: act within daysCOOL — flat or falling: watch, no rush

“Records no token” means the snapshot's record carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol — not that the protocol has none. The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling: the board ranks 200 early protocols below it too.
